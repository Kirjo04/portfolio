Overview

I worked with one other person to create a tutorial to teach users how to do something. We had to think about the design principles that we were learning (i.e. Fitts's Law, Hick's Law, Signaling Principle, etc.) and create the tutorial considering each principle or law. For example, in consideration of Signaling Principle, we included a table of contents to show the organization of content.
